Simple, beautiful diagrams.

An infinite whiteboard for flowcharts, sketches and ideas, with pressure-sensitive pen support. Free and open source, for macOS and Windows.

macOS (Apple Silicon & Intel) · Windows (x64 & ARM)

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Shapes that look good

Rectangles, pills, diamonds, databases and more, with eleven color styles that always match.

Smart connectors

Curved, straight or elbow lines that stick to shapes and follow them. Add labels and arrows.

Pen & highlighter

Pressure-sensitive ink with smoothing, a highlighter and an eraser, including your pen's eraser end.

Sticky notes & text

Drop notes, double-click anywhere to type, paste images and keep everything on one infinite canvas.

Export to PNG & SVG

Share a crisp image or a vector file of the whole board or just your selection.

Light, dark & your language

Follows your system theme, speaks English and Spanish, and is easy to translate to more languages.

Made for pens

Sketch like it's paper.

  • Stroke width follows your pen pressure.
  • Palm rejection while the pen touches the screen.
  • The eraser end of Surface Pen or Wacom just works.
  • Pen always draws: your pen draws while a finger or the mouse selects.
Everything at hand

Style it in a click.

  • An inspector that only shows what fits your selection.
  • Quick-connect handles create the next step for you.
  • Alignment guides, undo, layers and duplicate with ⌥/Alt.
  • Save boards as .drawnie files and open as many windows as you like.
